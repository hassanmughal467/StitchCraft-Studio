# Operational claims that need owner confirmation

These statements appear (or would appear) on public or legal pages. They are process language, not invented prices. Confirm or rewrite before treating them as contractual fact.

| Claim | Where it appears | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Based in Pakistan, serving US, UK, Australia and worldwide | About, Contact, footer | Confirm |
| Production starts after proof approval and payment | Service pages, quote success | Confirm sequence and payment timing |
| Shipping time is separate from production time | Service pages, shipping | Confirm |
| Requested dates are not confirmed until written on the quotation | Quote form, confirmations | Confirm |
| Digital files delivered by download | Digital service pages | Confirm |
| Physical products shipped with tracking | Physical service pages | Confirm |
| Artwork used only to prepare the quote | Quote page | Confirm retention and deletion |
| Approved files stay on record for reorders | Homepage, trade | Confirm retention period |
| Adjustments that keep the same artwork/size/placement are included | Service revision copy | Confirm revision count |
| A new size, garment or redesigned logo is quoted separately | Service revision copy | Confirm |
| Refund / remake wording | `/refund-policy` | Legal review |
| Privacy, cookies, terms, accessibility | Matching legal pages | Legal review |
| Data retention period | Privacy | Not published as a number |
| Artwork deletion process | Privacy | Confirm |
| Customs and duties responsibility | Shipping | Confirm |
| Payment methods and deposit | Not published | Owner input |
| Starting prices, minimums, rush fees | Commercial config (all null) | Owner input |

Do not publish a number, discount, minimum or guarantee until it is entered in `src/lib/config/business.ts` or the matching env var.
